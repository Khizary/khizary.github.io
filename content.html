II.Research & Projects

Selected work

Abstract accepted, 2026 · later withdrawn

STARK / SCOUT CLIP conditioning-vector compression for one-shot federated learning

In one-shot federated learning, clients upload CLIP text embeddings that condition a diffusion model on the server. SCOUT compresses each token vector adaptively, keeping the fewest high-magnitude dimensions that preserve a target cosine similarity. Stable Diffusion's cross-attention depends on direction rather than scale, so generation quality holds while upload volume falls, with no per-dataset tuning.

The abstract was accepted; we chose not to take the paper through to full submission and withdrew it.

Co-authored with Ali Azhar · OSCAR-style synthesis · CLIP, BLIP, Stable Diffusion · NICO++, DomainNet
